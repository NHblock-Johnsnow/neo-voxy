Neo Voxy 更新日志 / Changelog

## 0.4.6（1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版）— 2026-09-09

### 简体中文

- 新增 FramedBlocks 远景兼容：将方块实体驱动的主/副伪装、强化状态、可折叠偏移及特殊模型数据保存为 Voxy 持久化变体，并在 LOD 烘焙时恢复完整模型数据与伪装染色。
- 区段缺少方块实体（例如服务端流式远景数据）时会复用同一 Voxy 世界缓存中的 FramedBlocks 变体，避免远处结构在重新摄取后退回空框架或错误材质。
- 联动配置新增“远景框架方块”开关和独立距离限制；机械动力及其航空学、交错电网附属联动按顺序排列并统一命名。

### English

- Added distant FramedBlocks compatibility. Block-entity-driven primary/secondary camos, reinforcement, collapsible offsets, and special model data are stored as persistent Voxy variants and restored for LOD baking and camo tinting.
- FramedBlocks variants are reused from the same Voxy world cache when a streamed section has no block entities, preventing distant structures from reverting to empty frames or incorrect materials after re-ingestion.
- Added an independent FramedBlocks LOD toggle and distance limit, and reordered/renamed Create and its Aeronautics and PowerGrid integration groups.

- Coarse LODs no longer expand one isolated fluid sample into a full voxel, and transparent texture samples use a stable fluid-colour fallback.
- The 1.20.1 Forge release artifact now explicitly includes `mc1.20.1` in its filename.

## 0.4.5-beta.2（1.21.1 NeoForge 联动版）— 2026-09-06

### 简体中文

- 新增 Simulated 激光指示器远景光束：保留动态方向、命中长度、颜色、红石强度与末端渐弱，并在原版方块实体渲染距离外继续显示。
- 新增独立的 LOD 半透明绘制阶段，使激光正确使用 Voxy 地形深度，并接入 Iris 光影的透明 G-buffer；交界处保留安全重叠以避免闪烁。
- 新增远景激光开关与距离选项；仅安装 Simulated 时注册采集和渲染逻辑。PowerGrid 兼容状态更新为完全兼容。
- 将远景激光选项归入“航空学”，新增“小方块”和“模拟殖民地”远景开关与距离选项，并补充“交错电网”中文分组名称。
- 修复远景激光高于 LOD 地形、以天空为背景时被合成阶段裁掉的问题；光束现在写入 LOD 深度，可完整显示到末端。
- 修复水与岩浆在粗 LOD 中表面缩进被随层级放大，导致顶部缺面、侧面高出或形成深色竖墙的问题；顶面和侧面上缘现在共享固定世界尺度的流体高度。降采样也不再把单个孤立流体样本扩张为整个粗体素。
- 为水与岩浆的各个流动液位状态编码独立的 LOD 高度；LOD 0 保留原版液位轮廓，粗层级按状态恢复液面，并在相邻不同液位之间补齐连接侧壁，同时以微小顶面重叠消除网格缝和下层方块外露。
- 流动液体的内部高度差侧壁与最外围侧壁现在均严格截止到对应液位；保留原版斜顶面的同时，修复液位之间的空洞和外围侧面过高。
- 液位过渡面现在携带较低一侧的高度，只绘制两侧液面之间的差值区间，消除内部整墙重叠和残余缺面；同时修复草径、耕地等非满方块错误遮挡邻接完整侧面而产生的缝隙。
- 统一流体顶面与侧面的高度基准，不再混用邻域烘焙斜面和状态液位；取消对下落状态的无条件满高处理，修复仍然存在的内部横向空洞和外围细长高墙。
- 水平流体边界改为逐邻接生成，不再依赖会错位合并的 X/Z 流体扫描器；同高流体、液位台阶、空气边界和实体遮挡分别处理，以优先保证流体拓扑完整。
- 修复 X 向流体侧面把 Z 宽度误作 Y 高度的问题；X/Z 两个方向现在分别按各自面内坐标裁剪，消除满高细柱、窄片和对应缺口。
- 修复竖直流柱中同种流体因液位状态不同而生成内部水平面的错误；上方存在同种流体时按满高连接，不再在每个方块层之间留下细缝。
- 水与岩浆顶面改为按原版规则对邻接液位加权计算四角高度，并由 GPU 重建连续斜坡；顶面与外围梯形侧面共享角点，同种流体之间不再生成多余的高度差侧墙。
- 统一正负 X/Z 朝向及 32 方块区段边界的流体角点顺序；区段外侧面也改用四角梯形，并读取相邻区段液位，修复特定观察方向下侧面抬升和边界小缝。

### English

- Added distant Simulated laser-pointer beams, preserving dynamic direction, hit length, colour, redstone strength, and end falloff beyond block-entity render distance.
- Added a dedicated LOD translucent stage so lasers use Voxy terrain depth and the Iris translucent G-buffer, with a small safe handoff overlap to prevent flicker.
- Added independent distant-laser and distance options. Capture and rendering register only when Simulated is installed. PowerGrid is now marked fully compatible.
- Moved distant-laser options into Aeronautics, added Little Tiles and Domum Ornamentum enable/distance options, and localized the PowerGrid group name in Chinese.
- Fixed distant lasers being discarded against the sky above LOD terrain. Beams now write LOD depth and remain visible through their full length.
- Fixed water and lava surface indentation scaling with coarse LOD levels, which caused missing tops, over-tall sides, and dark vertical walls. Top faces and side rims now share a world-scale fluid height, and isolated single fluid samples no longer expand into a full coarse voxel.
- Encoded a dedicated LOD height for every flowing water and lava level. LOD 0 keeps the native profile, while coarser levels reconstruct state-specific surfaces, add connecting walls between adjacent levels, and use a minute cap overlap to eliminate grid seams and exposed blocks below.
- Internal step walls and outer walls of flowing fluids now end at their exact state height at every LOD level, preserving native sloped caps while fixing holes between levels and over-tall perimeter faces.
- Fluid transition faces now carry the lower neighbour height and draw only the interval between both surfaces, removing overlapping full walls and residual holes. Partial-height blocks such as dirt paths and farmland no longer incorrectly occlude a neighbour's complete side face.
- Unified fluid cap and wall height calculations instead of mixing neighbourhood-baked slopes with state heights. Falling states are no longer forced to full height unconditionally, fixing the remaining horizontal holes and thin over-tall perimeter walls.
- Horizontal fluid boundaries are now generated per adjacency instead of using the misaligned X/Z fluid scan mergers. Equal surfaces, level steps, air boundaries, and solid occluders are handled separately to preserve correct fluid topology.
- Fixed X-facing fluid walls treating their Z span as the Y-height coordinate. X and Z faces now use their respective in-plane axis order, removing full-height posts, narrow strips, and the matching holes.
- Fixed internal horizontal faces being generated between vertically stacked fluids of the same kind when their states differed. A matching fluid above now makes the column continuous and full-height between block layers.
- Water and lava caps now use vanilla-style weighted neighbouring heights at all four corners and reconstruct a continuous slope on the GPU. Caps and perimeter trapezoid walls share their corner heights, while adjacent fluids of the same kind no longer create redundant step walls.
- Unified fluid-corner ordering across positive and negative X/Z faces and 32-block section boundaries. Outer section walls now use the same four-corner trapezoids and neighbouring-section heights, fixing raised walls and small seams visible from specific directions.

### English

- Added distant rendering for static LittleTiles microblocks. Geometry is captured once during section ingestion, quantized into sparse 1/8-block surface meshes, and keeps block textures, LittleTiles colours, biome tinting, lighting, and internal-face culling.
- Microblock meshes persist with the Voxy world cache and upload at most two changed sections per tick. Without LittleTiles, no related renderer is registered.
- Fixed persisted LittleTiles meshes being deleted when block entities were not ready during world entry, and removed the invisible handoff band with a conservative circular vanilla/LOD overlap.
- Fixed LittleTiles distant meshes existing only in memory and disappearing after `/voxy reload` or rejoining in place. Snapshot updates are now persisted defensively and checkpointed before reload, logout, and same-world storage replacement.
- Fixed LittleTiles distant meshes being forced to full brightness. They now use captured sky/block light with the live vanilla light map, skip translucent LOD fading, and perform a hard 3-D handoff at the fade start.
- Updated the integrations edition to 0.4.2-beta.1, standardized the mod metadata and configuration-screen name across all four editions as `Neo-Voxy`, and refreshed both compatibility tables.
